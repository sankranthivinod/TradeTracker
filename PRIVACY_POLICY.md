# Privacy Policy for Trade Tracker

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026  

Thank you for using **Trade Tracker** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience while managing your trading activities. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the Trade Tracker mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Personal Information
**Trade Tracker does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identification information.** We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

### B. App Data & Local Storage
All data entered into Trade Tracker—including trade logs, stock/asset symbols, buy/sell prices, quantity, dates, strategies, and notes—is stored **100% locally on your device** using an encrypted local SQLite/Room database. 
- Your financial data **never leaves your device**.
- We do **not** operate external cloud servers or databases that collect or store your trade history.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Trade Tracker is built as an offline-first tool.
- We do **not** integrate third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
- We do **not** use third-party advertising networks or tracking SDKs.
- No data is sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing partners.

---

## 3. Network & Internet Access

Trade Tracker does not require or request internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`). The App operates entirely offline without sending or receiving background data.

---

## 4. Data Control & Deletion

Since all data is stored strictly on your local device:
- **Modification & Deletion:** You can edit or delete individual trade records directly within the App at any time.
- **Complete Data Removal:** Clearing the App's data via your device settings (`Settings > Apps > Trade Tracker > Storage > Clear Data`) or uninstalling the App will permanently delete all saved trade records and settings.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Trade Tracker does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because the App collects no personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations regarding minors.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or Play Store policy guidelines. Any updates will be posted directly within this document with a revised "Last Updated" date.
